# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how SmartPlate AI ("we," "us," "our") collects,
uses, and shares information when you use our website and related services
(the "Service").

> **Note:** This is a starting draft written for a small, self-run app. It is
> not legal advice. Before you launch, have a lawyer review it — especially
> if you'll have users in the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA), or other
> places with their own privacy laws, since those require specific language
> and rights we've only sketched here.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your name,
email address, and password. Your password is never stored in plain text —
it's hashed before it's saved, and we cannot look it up or recover it for
you if you forget it.

**Meal plan data.** The meals you add to your plan, the days you assign
them to, and your resulting shopping list. This is stored so your plan is
there when you come back, and so you can share it if you choose to.

**Payment information.** If you upgrade to Plus, your payment is handled
directly by our payment processor, Flutterwave, which handles both card
and mobile money payments (MTN MoMo, Orange Money, M-Pesa, Airtel Money
and others, depending on your country). We do not receive or
store your full card number, mobile money PIN, or bank details — those go
straight to the processor. We do receive confirmation that a payment
succeeded, which plan you're on, and your billing cycle (monthly or
yearly).

**AI meal planner.** If you use the AI planner, we send your stated
preferences (cuisine, number of meals, dietary focus) to Anthropic's Claude
API to generate suggestions. We do not send your name, email, or payment
information as part of that request.

**Technical information.** Standard technical data your browser sends to
any website, such as IP address and browser type, may be logged by our
hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

**Local storage.** Some information — your session token, and, if you're
using the site without an account, a demo version of your plan — is stored
in your browser's local storage rather than on our servers.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Create and secure your account
- Save and display your meal plan and shopping list
- Process payments and manage your subscription
- Generate meal suggestions through the AI planner
- Respond to you if you contact us for support
- Keep the Service secure and working correctly

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to serve
third-party advertising.

## 3. Sharing your plan

If you use the "share" feature, you choose where your plan goes — for
example, a WhatsApp message, a Facebook post, or a copied link. We don't
automatically post or send anything on your behalf. Anyone you send a share
link to can view the meals in that plan.

## 4. Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

- **Flutterwave**, our payment processor, to process your subscription
  or cookbook payment (covering both card and mobile money payments).
- **Anthropic**, to generate AI meal plan suggestions, as described above.
- **Our hosting provider**, to run the website and store data.
- **Law enforcement or others**, only if required by law, or to protect
  the rights, safety, or property of SmartPlate AI or our users.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active.
If you delete your account (or ask us to), we'll delete your name, email,
password hash, and meal plan data within [30] days, except where we're
required to keep records (for example, payment records for tax purposes).

## 6. Your choices

- **Access and correction.** You can view and update your name and plan
  from your account settings.
- **Deletion.** You can ask us to delete your account and associated data
  at any time by contacting us (see below).
- **Your Plus status.** View your current plan, or return your account to
  Free before a paid period ends, from the "Manage Plus billing" option in
  your account menu. See our Terms of Service for how billing works.

## 7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect
information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we'll remove it.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including
password hashing and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage
or transmission is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute
security.

## 9. International users

If you use the Service from outside the country where our servers are
located, your information will be transferred to and processed in that
country. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material
changes, we'll notify you by posting a notice on the Service or by email.
